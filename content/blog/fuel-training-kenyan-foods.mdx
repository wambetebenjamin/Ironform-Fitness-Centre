Performance nutrition does not need to be imported or complicated. Familiar foods can cover the carbohydrates, protein, colour and hydration your training demands.

## Build the plate

Use ugali, rice, potatoes or matoke as a useful energy base. Add beans, eggs, fish, chicken or lean meat for protein, then build colour with sukuma wiki and seasonal vegetables.

## Time it simply

A balanced meal two to three hours before training works well for most members. If you are short on time, choose a lighter snack such as fruit and yoghurt.

## Start with consistency

The best plan is one you can repeat. Focus on regular meals, enough water and sensible portions before worrying about fine details.
