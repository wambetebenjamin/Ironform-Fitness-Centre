Progress is easier to repeat when every session has a clear job. A balanced four-day week gives you enough stimulus to adapt without making every day feel like a test.

## The four-day structure

Start with two full-body strength days, one conditioning day and one skill or mobility day. Keep a rest or light walking day between your hardest efforts whenever your calendar allows.

## Earn the next step

Add load only after your reps are smooth and repeatable. If technique changes under fatigue, hold the weight and improve the quality first. Your coach can help you choose the right progression.

## Recovery counts

Plan sleep, hydration and meals with the same intent as your sessions. The work creates the signal; recovery is where your body responds.
